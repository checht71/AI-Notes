The Markov property is the memoryless property of a stochastic process. That means that history is not relevant at all to its decision making.

We can toss away all of the historical data and make a prediction just based on the most recent data. This is how you say that mathematically:
$$ P(s_4| s_3 s_2 s_1) = P(s_4|s_3)$$
$$ P_{ss'} = P(s'|s)$$
