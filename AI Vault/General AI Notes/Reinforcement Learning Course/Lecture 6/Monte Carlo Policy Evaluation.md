Monte Carlo policy evaluation is a method used in reinforcement learning to estimate the value of a policy by averaging the returns observed from multiple episodes of experience. 
The sample mean of the Monte Carlo Policy Evaluation converges to the true mean.

Basically you just run through a simulation of each policy, average the returns, and then pick the maximum one.
