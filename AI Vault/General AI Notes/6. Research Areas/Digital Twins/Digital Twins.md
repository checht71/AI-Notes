A digital twin is a virtual prototype of an entire working system in a real world environment.
DTs are used to manage the entire life cycle of products. This is all to make them more efficient. DTs also integrate several engineering disciplines.

A Digital Twin is NOT a Digital model. There must be something from real life that you are directly uploading to create a digital twin.

You can use AI to create and analyze virtual assets and test things out that don't even exist.
You can simulate an entire environment.
